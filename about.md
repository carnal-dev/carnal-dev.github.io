I build things and write down what actually happened.

The through-line here is machine learning learned the long way: from-scratch implementations,
paper replications with honest gap analyses, simulation, and the systems underneath it all.
Training logs over hot takes. If a post claims a number, the repo to reproduce it is linked.

The aesthetic debt is to old engravings and mission-control screens: instruments, hard edges,
and one ember-colored light in a dark room.

Elsewhere: [GitHub](https://github.com/carnal-dev), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/carnaldev),
[X](https://x.com/carnaldev). The handle is a pseudonym and the work stands on its own.
