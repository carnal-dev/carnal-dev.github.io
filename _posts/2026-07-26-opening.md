I am going to start openly writing about my experiences as a developer and the things I learn,
starting today. I am laying out a serious roadmap for myself to build upon my prior knowledge of
working with my own personal private operating systems and machine learning systems and more,
like Atlas, an autonomous research system I have been building that does its own research,
commits to predictions with dates and confidence attached, and measures itself against what
actually happens so it gets sharper the longer it runs.

My writing is not very professional and I am not incredible at articulating myself, but I will
make the best effort I can to display my skills and the things I learn, and show my process and
day to day life slowly. No promises on consistency in uploads, but when I do make progress I
will certainly be here to post it first.
