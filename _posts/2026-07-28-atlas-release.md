Last post I wrote about why Atlas ended up pulling me away from finance and toward the engine itself. I said I would clean up the data and put it on github and I did. The full dataset that I decided I could release without compromising any personal information or publishing illegal data are available at
**[github.com/carnal-dev/atlas-data](https://github.com/carnal-dev/atlas-data)**.
There are some interesting bits and pieces in there for sure, the Stale Tickers, although stale do hold a decent research summary of some interesting opportunistic companies that if you care to check out you should. There is a lot of scraped data also in the form of the SEC filings and such that I put onto there since why not if its legal and open. If anyone ever wants the actual scripts or some resources from Atlas they could reach out to me at my email ml@carnal-dev.com to inquire about it and I'd be happy to release it to them, I just rather not put any of those scripts out there honestly as it would be a lot of work to do for no one to actual go out to seek them. (Only person reading this is Sam as far as I know right now.) There are backtester scripts, factor evaluation, factor study, event study, edge measurement, SEC scripts, filing alerts and more that I would be comfortable sharing if anyone were interested in it.

I also might have misspoken in my last post, I never did fully close the loop, it still required one piece of actual input to re-run the engine again after around 6 hours. (I just had to make sure nothing broke and hit run again.) So I am confident the loop is functional, I just haven't run it on its own fully so if you do inquire to learn about how I did loop it be warned it is not confirmed to be fully functioning, just in theory.

All of the data was collected between February 28 - July 2nd 2026, the engine hasn't run since and as I said before my attention has moved on and this is probably the last I will speak of it to be completely honest, I just didn't want to have vague post syndrome and let the thing I mentioned in the opening stay a mystery, hope you find the data interesting and if you wish to inquire more do not hesitate to reach out!
