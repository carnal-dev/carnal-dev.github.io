This is a working notebook, kept in public. The subject is machine learning learned the hard
way: by hand, from first principles, with the math derived before the library call and the
failure documented next to the result.

The rules, so future posts can be held to them:

1. **Every claim ships with its evidence.** A number gets a repo, a plot gets its script, a
   speedup gets the benchmark you can run yourself.
2. **Failures get written up at the same length as wins.** A replication that hits 80% of the
   paper's result, with an honest account of the gap, beats a clean demo every time.
3. **Something ships every week.** Small is fine. Silent is not.

The near-term queue: from-scratch fundamentals (an autograd engine, gradient descent you can
watch, a transformer built line by line), then outward into simulation, reinforcement learning,
and the physics-flavored corners of ML where I intend to live.

The light is on. More soon.
